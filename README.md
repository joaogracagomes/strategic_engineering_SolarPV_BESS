# Strategic Engineering Analysis of Solar PV + BESS Deployment

**Original research code accompanying:**
**_A Strategic Engineering Analysis of Flexible and Robust Deployment of Solar PV Power Plants Coupled with Battery Energy Storage Systems_**  
João Graça Gomes, Michel-Alexandre Cardin*, Billy Wu  
Dyson School of Design Engineering, Imperial College London, UK  
\*Corresponding author: m.cardin@imperial.ac.uk  

---

## 📄 Overview

This repository contains the **original codebase** used to support the analysis presented in the above research work.
The project explores how **battery energy storage systems (BESS)** can be optimally deployed alongside **solar photovoltaic (PV)** systems under uncertainty. Traditional planning approaches often assume fixed conditions and fail to account for real-world variability such as:
- Grid constraints  
- Power output fluctuations  
- Equipment degradation and failures  
- Market uncertainty  

This work instead adopts a **Strategic Engineering approach**, treating uncertainty as a *core design feature* rather than a constraint.

---

## ⚙️ Key Contributions

- Introduces a **flexibility-driven design framework** for PV + BESS systems  
- Applies **real options analysis** to energy infrastructure planning  
- Uses **Monte Carlo simulations** (Geometric Brownian Motion) to model uncertainty  
- Compares three deployment strategies:
  - Fixed  
  - Phased  
  - Flexible  

---

## 📊 Main Findings

- Flexible strategies can reduce:
  - **LCOE** (Levelized Cost of Electricity) by up to **24.6%**  
  - **LCOS** (Levelized Cost of Storage) by up to **19%**  
- **Modular initial designs + adaptive expansion** consistently increase value under uncertainty  
- Flexibility becomes more valuable as:
  - Uncertainty increases  
  - Learning effects improve costs  
  - Degradation rates rise  

---

## 🌍 Case Study

The methodology is applied to a **Portugal-based deployment scenario**, evaluating multi-site PV + BESS configurations under uncertain market and technical conditions.
